Meet the Puppies

About Us

Welcome to Orchard Pups! We are a small, dedicated family-owned business nestled in the beautiful countryside of Rochester, NY. Our journey began with a simple love for dogs and a desire to provide families with healthy, well-socialized, and truly happy companions.

Every puppy at Orchard Pups is raised right in our home, surrounded by the laughter of our children and the quiet peace of the orchard. We don't just breed dogs; we nurture future family members. From the very first breath to the day they join your home, our puppies receive around-the-clock care, premium nutrition, and endless snuggles.

Whether you're looking for a playful Golden Retriever or a gentle Goldendoodle, we’re here to help you find your perfect match. Thank you for letting us be a small part of your family's story!
